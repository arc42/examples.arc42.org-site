Everything it shows comes from `sys` — the system's index.md front matter. `index` is the tile's position in the sorted collection, used only as a catalogue number. Colour: NONE. Per the design decision recorded in DESIGN.md, tiles are neutral paper and the domain is carried as text. The hook for changing that later is the `data-domain` attribute below: giving domains colour is a CSS-only change (one attribute selector setting one custom property, the `--cat` mechanism quality.arc42.org exports), with no template edit and no content migration. Do not add colour without also giving it a legend — on this site colour would be the ONLY category signal, and colour must never carry meaning alone. LABELS. This tile used to caption every row — MAIN GOAL, STRATEGIC DECISIONS, TECHNOLOGIES — in the same small grey uppercase, so one texture repeated four times down the card and each caption carried as much visual weight as the content beside it. The tile read as a form. The captions are gone from the page and kept for screen readers on the list that needs one: a bulleted clause under a tagline reads as "decision" to anyone looking at it, and says nothing at all to anyone listening. The whole tile is clickable via the stretched-link pattern: a single real around the title, expanded by a pseudo-element. This keeps exactly one link per tile in the accessibility tree and in the tab order — a tile with a link on the title AND the wrapper announces every example twice. {%- endcomment -%}
