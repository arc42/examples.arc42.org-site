{%- comment -%} One dashboard tile. Everything it shows comes from `sys` — the system's index.md front matter. Colour: NONE. Per the design decision recorded in DESIGN.md, tiles are neutral paper and the domain is carried as text. The hook for changing that later is the `data-domain` attribute below: giving domains colour is a CSS-only change (one attribute selector setting one custom property, the `--cat` mechanism quality.arc42.org exports), with no template edit and no content migration. Do not add colour without also giving it a legend — on this site colour would be the ONLY category signal, and colour must never carry meaning alone. The whole tile is clickable via the stretched-link pattern: a single real around the title, expanded by a pseudo-element. This keeps exactly one link per tile in the accessibility tree and in the tab order — a tile with a link on the title AND the wrapper announces every example twice. {%- endcomment -%}
