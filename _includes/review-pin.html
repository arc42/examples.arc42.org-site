{%- comment -%} The review pin — the one graphical symbol shared by every review note and by the disclaimer on the system overview. The sameness is the point: the symbol is what ties a note in section 7 back to the sentence on the overview that says what these notes are. Draw it nowhere else and draw nothing else here. A ball-head pin at 45° — the pinned-note shadow's metaphor made literal — with an AMBER head: the family's sticky-note colour, spine segment 3, taking its registered second surface job here (_sass/_tokens.scss). The dark ring is what makes the light amber read on a light wash: amber vs --ground-deep measures 9.4:1, while bare amber on --wash-calm is 1.3:1. Decorative either way: the visible text label beside it carries the meaning (a purely visual distinction must never be the only signal), so it is aria-hidden and WCAG 1.4.11 does not apply. The needle inherits currentColor, so the context sets its ink. {%- endcomment -%}
