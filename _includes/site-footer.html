{%- comment -%} The site footer — docs.arc42.org's and faq.arc42.org's, cloned. This replaces a footer invented for this site: an editorial lead paragraph restating the masthead tagline, a two-column sitemap of eight links, and a legal line, on a 410px dark slab — 34% of the home page, and the last thing a reader saw on every one of them. What was actually wrong with it was not its height. It omitted the INNOQ sponsor mark and the maintainer credit that every other family site carries, and it omitted Contact and Status entirely. Those are obligations, not styling. The family already has a settled component for all of it, so this site now uses that component rather than a third arrangement of the same facts. The class names are the family's (`.site-footer`, not `.ex-footer`): per the Same-Name Rule a family name is a published interface, and this matches the family spec, so it takes the family name. Imprint and Contact point at arc42.org, which is where they live — this site has no such pages of its own and should not grow duplicates of them. One line is site-specific and stays: the per-example licence note. These are other people's systems reproduced here, and that is a fact about this site which no other family site has to state. {%- endcomment -%}
