      {%- comment -%} THE TITLE LINK IS THE DOCUMENTATION, always, and it is the reason the entry exists. Until 2026-08-15 it was also the only link an entry could carry. It is not any more: a note may link inline, and `links:` prints a short labelled block at the foot. What did NOT change is that this one points at the document itself. An entry whose title link goes to a project's home page instead has quietly become an advertisement. Every link on this page, here and below, obeys the same two rules. NO `rel`. It carried `noopener noreferrer` until 2026-08-11. `noopener` does nothing without target="_blank", which these links deliberately do not have. `noreferrer` did do something: it stripped the Referer header, so a project we send readers to could not see that the referral came from here. Sending it is the courtesy, and the default strict-origin-when-cross-origin sends the origin and nothing more. NO target="_blank", and DO NOT add one to make a rel meaningful again. Opening in the same tab is the decision (WCAG 3.2.5, argued at the host line below); the rel was the leftover. The corollary is that a reader must be able to see where a link goes BEFORE clicking it, which is why the host is printed under the title, why the block at the foot uses the bare host as its link text, and why the schema requires the text of an inline link in a note to name its destination. {%- endcomment -%}
