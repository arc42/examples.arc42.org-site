      {%- comment -%} Exactly one link per entry, on the title, and no `rel`. It carried `noopener noreferrer` until 2026-08-11. `noopener` does nothing without target="_blank", which these links deliberately do not have. `noreferrer` did do something: it stripped the Referer header, so a project we send readers to could not see that the referral came from here. Sending it is the courtesy, and the default strict-origin-when-cross-origin sends the origin and nothing more. DO NOT add target="_blank" to make a rel meaningful again. Opening in the same tab is the decision (WCAG 3.2.5, argued at the host line below); the rel was the leftover. {%- endcomment -%}
