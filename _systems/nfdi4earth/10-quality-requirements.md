Not documented as a section of its own — no quality tree, no scenarios, in
either the PDF or the consortium's
[online version](https://nfdi4earth.pages.rwth-aachen.de/architecture/architecture-docs/).
The three quality goals NFDI4Earth commits to are stated in
[section 1](../01-introduction-and-goals/), taken from ISO 25010:2011:
**functional suitability**, **maintainability** and **usability**.

The original notes that the architecture is evolving and that the
*prioritization* of these goals is meant to be re-evaluated regularly, which is
why they are kept as goals rather than pinned down as measurable scenarios.
