== Risks and Technical Debts

.Technical Risks
[cols="1,3,3,5"]
|===
| *ID* | *Problem/Risk* | *Impact* | *Description*
| R1 
| Integration of Third Party Tools | Increased expenses when integrating the tools. | The Third Party Tools are more or less black boxes which should be integrated in our SSO infrastructure. Maybe some adoption of the tools are necessary or some facades around the tools must be implemented.
| R2 | UI/UX of Third Party Tools | Different UI/UX behavior of the third party tools. | The adoption of the UI of the Third Party Tool is maybe not possible or very restricted. Sometimes the tools offers theming to customize the UI.
|===
