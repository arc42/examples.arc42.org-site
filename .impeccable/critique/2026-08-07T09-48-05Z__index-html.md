## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored at the leaf, generic at the root.**

The interior pages could not be lifted onto another site. The twelve-cell section grid is arc42's own shape laid flat in oversized Libre Caslon numerals. The rail breakpoint is derived from the reading measure, not from a device convention, with the arithmetic recorded at the breakpoint. The collapsed rail is a snap-scrolling pill strip, and the include documents why `<details open>` was rejected. The provenance block — written by / licence / original / imported, under the pinned-note shadow — is the site's editorial position rendered as a component.

The dashboard is the most generic artifact on the site. Search box plus `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(19rem, 1fr))` plus neutral bordered cards is what every documentation index ships. The site's own thesis — *one structure, so you can read any two of them against each other* — is stated in prose on the home page and then has zero expression anywhere in the interface.

**Deterministic scan.** Source pass over `_includes _layouts _pages index.md 404.html`: exit 0, zero findings. Built pass over `_site`: one warning, `side-tab` at `style.css:1` (`border-left: 4px solid var(--rule)`, from `_sass/_content.scss:109`). **False positive** — it is a deliberately grey `blockquote` quotation rule, not a brand-coloured accent tab on a card, and the source says so.

**Live injection succeeded** on all six routes (live-server on :8400, since stopped). 24 findings at 1440 wide, 0 at 390, 2 at 320. All 24 at 1440 are `line-length`; two of them measure **~159 characters per line** on the home page. One `text-overflow` at 320 (a bare GitHub URL overflowing its container by 78px) and one `edge-flush-cards` on a `<table>`, the latter a false positive.

Two site claims fail against the pixels, and both are mine:

1. `/contribute/` and `DESIGN.md` say the site's only colour is the spine. `_sass/_masthead.scss:106` sets `text-decoration-color: var(--arc42-amber)` on the current nav item. `_tokens.scss` records amber's jobs as "spine segment 3 · focus ring on the band". This is a third, unregistered job. It looks good — the fix is to register it, not remove it.
2. `assets/js/filter.js` says "a control that cannot work must not be on screen" and sets `hidden` in the markup. There is no `[hidden]` rule in `_sass/`, and `.ex-filter { display: flex }` beats the UA `[hidden] { display: none }`. The attribute is inert.

## Overall Impression

The bones are the best thing here and they are genuinely good: the chrome discipline is complete, nothing generator-default survived, focus rings are ground-aware and measured (14.12:1 on paper, 8.15:1 amber on the band), `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured with zero elements animating under it, every `<nav>` has a unique accessible name, every page has exactly one `<h1>` and no skipped levels, and the stretched-link tile ships without the duplicate-link bug that pattern usually carries.

The single biggest opportunity is that the dashboard — the primary surface, the domain apex — has no idea of its own. Both of your notes point at it, and one of them is pointing at the wrong thing.

## What's Working

**The twelve-cell section grid.** It maps a known abstraction onto a spatial layout at 1:1 — no metaphor, no legend needed — and the oversized Caslon numerals carry the whole hierarchy without a single hue, on a site that has none to spend. This is the site's best component and its most reusable idea.

**The collapsed rail is a real responsive design, not a hidden desktop one.** Below 940px it becomes a horizontally snap-scrolling strip where the current section stays expanded; the partial clipping at the right edge doubles as the scroll affordance.

**Provenance is answered before it is asked.** Written-by, licence, original URL, import date, and "these are snapshots and are not maintained in step with the original". For an architect deciding whether to trust and cite an example, that is exactly the anxiety being answered, and the pinned-note shadow gives it the right physical register.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The filter is a visible dead control

`.ex-filter { display: flex }` overrides the `hidden` attribute — there is no `[hidden]` rule in the stylesheet — so a 1352×44 labelled search input renders on the home page in all states. `filter.js` then early-returns at `if (total < 2)`, so nothing is ever wired. Typing `qqqq` with real keystrokes changes nothing; the `role="status"` count is a 0×0 empty box that never announces. It is tab stop 8 of 19.

**Why it matters:** the only interactive control on the site does nothing, sits in the keyboard path, and fails silently. It also proves the site's own progressive-enhancement claim false — and even after real examples land and `filter.js` wires up, the input stays visible-and-dead during the window before the deferred script runs, on every page load.

**Fix:** add `[hidden] { display: none !important; }` to `_sass/_reset.scss` (the family reset is the right home — this class of bug recurs anywhere a component sets `display` and something else sets `hidden`), and drop the `total < 2` early return to a `total < 2` *guard on wiring* that still unhides. Then decide whether one example deserves a filter at all.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] The footer is not a family member — and the gap is attribution, not styling

Measured against `docs.arc42.org-site/_includes/site-footer.html` and `quality.arc42.org-site/_includes/footer.html`, the family footer is a settled three-part component: the **INNOQ sponsor mark**, one inline middot row (`Contact · Status · Imprint + Privacy · GitHub`), and the credit line **"Created by Gernot Starke, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Supported by Kröner & Starke."** This site carries **none of the three**. It invents an editorial lead paragraph and a two-column sitemap instead, omits Contact and Status entirely, and lands at 410px — 34% of the home page, and the site's *end*, so it carries disproportionate weight in what a visitor remembers.

Your note was right and understated. This is not a tonal mismatch; INNOQ's sponsor mark and the maintainer credit are obligations every sibling honours, and they are missing. The lead paragraph is also near-verbatim duplication of the masthead tagline directly above the fold.

**Fix:** adopt the family component wholesale, under the family class name `.site-footer` — per the Same-Name Rule, a component that matches the family spec should carry the family name, and `.ex-footer` currently declares a deviation that isn't wanted. Keep exactly one site-specific addition: the per-example licence sentence, which is genuinely load-bearing here. Delete the lead paragraph. Expect ~150px instead of 410.

**Optional, and it costs an ADR:** reprising the spine along the footer's top edge would bracket the page in the site's own device and is the strongest cheap move available. But ADR-0008 registers the spine as "a 6px rule **under the masthead**", so this amends the device's definition rather than applying it. Worth doing, not worth doing quietly.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

### [P1] The home page's prose has no measure cap at all

`{{ content }}` in `_layouts/home.html` renders directly into `.ex-shell` (`--shell-max`), never into `.ex-prose` (`--measure`). Measured 117–130 characters per line at 1440 and 1920; the live detector independently reported ~159. Every other page on the site caps at ~68ch.

**Why it matters:** the 65–75ch reading measure is a *family constant*, defended in meta's DESIGN.md §2.4 and re-stated in this site's own DESIGN.md — and it is broken on the one page every visitor sees first. The masthead tagline right above it correctly caps at 46ch, which makes the contrast visible without measuring.

**Fix:** wrap `{{ content }}` in `.ex-prose`, or set the measure on the home content block directly.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`

### [P1] Sticky masthead overlaps every anchor target on a phone

`scroll-padding-top` is derived from `--bar-h: 56px`, but the masthead measures **128px at 390–430px wide** and **201px at 320px** — the brand row and nav row wrap, and `.ex-masthead--bar` has no width media query. Any in-page anchor, fragment link, or back-restoration lands 50–123px underneath an opaque band.

**Fix:** collapse the masthead to one row at ≤430px (dropping `arc42.org` from the phone nav is the cheapest way) so the 56px derivation holds. 128px of permanent sticky chrome on an 844px viewport is 15% of the screen for a nav nobody uses while reading.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

### [P2] The dashboard is one self-declared placeholder in a four-track grid

At 1440: four tracks, one occupant, 1080px of empty paper. The tile's own tagline reads "A placeholder, here to show what a tile and a documentation look like"; clicking it lands on a page whose first paragraph is "This is not a real system." A visitor cannot distinguish "under construction" from "empty".

I know real examples are coming, so this resolves itself — but it is worth naming because **every downstream judgement about tile design is unanswerable until it does.** A four-column grid with one occupant looks broken in any colour.

**Fix, for the interim:** state the count, and give the grid a second occupant that is honest — a "contribute an example" ghost tile in track 2, which is useful permanently and stops the row reading as a rendering failure.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable onboard`

### [P2] Below 940px the rail is twelve anonymous numerals, at 35×38px

`.ex-rail__item:not(.is-current) .ex-rail__name { display: none }`. Measured targets 34.9×37.6px — both dimensions under 44 — with adjacent gaps of 7.8px, 7.1px and 7.3px, all under the 8px minimum. This is the primary navigation of the whole document set on mobile, and it demands recall of arc42's ordering from the readers least likely to have it memorised.

**Fix:** truncate rather than hide — `max-width: 11ch; text-overflow: ellipsis` — so the strip reads `3 Context…  4 Solution…  5 Building…`. It already scrolls; making it longer costs nothing and converts recall into recognition while fixing the target size in the same change.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

### [P3] Two contrast failures and a stale-doc pair

`.ex-rail__num` on the **current** rail item measures **4.28:1** (`--muted` on `--wash-active`) — `--muted` was measured against `--paper` at 5.50:1 and never re-measured against the active fill. This is the Measured-Pair Rule failing in exactly the way the rule exists to prevent. The `·` separator in the section eyebrow measures 1.98:1 and, more to the point, is announced by screen readers — it needs `aria-hidden="true"` regardless of the ratio.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** lands on `/`, reads the site's premise three times in the first viewport (wordmark → H1 → tagline → intro), then meets one card that says it is a placeholder, clicks it, and reads "This is not a real system" followed by an `rm -rf` command. Jordan's entire first session is spent inside scaffolding. They also never learn how many examples exist. Then they type into the filter and nothing happens.

**Casey (mobile):** three failures at once on a section page at 390px — a 128px sticky masthead eating 15% of the viewport permanently, a rail of bare 35×38px numerals so choosing section 7 requires knowing what 7 is, and `scroll-padding-top: 78px` so any anchor lands under the band. On the system overview the twelve-cell grid stacks to ~650px of ladder, putting `Start reading →` below the fold twice over.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard):** the foundations are unusually strong — real skip link, ground-aware focus rings at 14.12:1 and 8.15:1, `aria-current` on nav/rail/breadcrumb, spine `aria-hidden`, zero focusable elements inside `aria-hidden`, zero images without alt. Four concrete breaks: two adjacent links both announced "Examples" both pointing at `/` (the brand link needs `aria-label="arc42 Examples — home"`); two duplicate adjacent "Sample System" links on the section page; `pre.highlight` scrolls 403px inside a 340px box with no `tabindex="0"`, so the truncated code line is unreachable by keyboard (WCAG 2.1.1); and the `<table>` has no `<caption>` and no `scope` on any `<th>`.

**And the one nobody wants to hear:** link identification rests entirely on a **2.49:1** underline. `--link` is `#2f2922` against body ink `#2a2e34` — visually the same near-black, by design — so colour carries nothing and the sole distinguisher is a 1px hairline in `--link-under #9b9895`, documented in `_tokens.scss` as "decorative only". That satisfies the letter of 1.4.1 but it is the real, unacknowledged cost of the no-hue decision, which DESIGN.md presents as a clean win. A darker *neutral* underline costs no hue and breaks no rule.

## On Your Two Notes

**"The footer doesn't fit the brand" — agreed, and it's worse than a fit problem.** See P1. Missing sponsor mark, missing maintainer credit, missing Contact and Status, plus an invented layout where the family has a settled component.

**"The system cards could use more visual spice" — the perception is right, the diagnosis isn't colour.** Look at the tile: `DEMONSTRATION` / title / tagline / `MAIN GOAL` / text / `STRATEGIC DECISIONS` / pills / `TECHNOLOGIES` / pills / rule / scale line. That is **four identical grey uppercase labels** repeating one texture down the card, each taking as much visual weight as the content beside it. The tile reads as a *form*, not a *specimen*. That — not the absence of pigment — is why it feels flat.

The disciplined fixes, in order of ratio:

1. **Cut three of the four labels.** Mono pills already read as technologies and word pills as decisions; the domain eyebrow already sits above the title. One label survives at most. This is a deletion, and it is the single biggest improvement available to the tile.
2. **Give the tile a focal mark.** A display-size Libre Caslon numeral or domain initial, in a wash tone, borrowing the section grid's best idea. Zero pigment, and it makes the tile read as a specimen card — precisely the metaphor the spine already claims.
3. **Vary size, not colour.** A featured example spanning two tracks with `grid-auto-flow: dense` gives the grid rhythm and structurally solves the lonely-row problem. This scales from 2 examples to 20.
4. **Only then, domain colour** via the existing `data-domain` hook and the spine tokens. Hold this until there are ≥6 examples across ≥4 domains: the moment a spine token *means* a domain it stops being decorative, loses ADR-0008's WCAG 1.4.11 exemption, and every segment needs 3:1 against its neighbours — plus a legend and a fixed domain vocabulary, which `domain:` being free text currently prevents.

Do 1 and 2 now. Do 3 when the second example lands. Do 4 by ADR or not at all.

## Minor Observations

- `index.md` sets `title: Architecture examples` while the H1 renders `site.title` "arc42 Examples" — the browser tab and the page disagree.
- No print stylesheet exists (`@media print` count: 0). A section page prints with the dark masthead, the spine, the 12-item rail and a breadcrumb on page 1, and a **410px solid dark-brown footer slab** on page 2 followed by half a blank page. Link URLs are not expanded. For a site whose subject is documents people print, this is a real gap.
- `.ex-tile:hover` leaves its lifted transform stuck after a tap on touch devices — guard with `@media (hover: hover)`.
- Horizontal overflow at 320px on two pages: `li.ex-tile` by 8px, and a bare GitHub URL by 29px (needs `overflow-wrap: anywhere`).
- `.ex-facts dt` is `--muted` on `--wash-calm` at **4.77:1** — passes AA by 0.27 and appears on every system page. The site's thinnest margin; worth knowing.
- `<main>` has no `tabindex="-1"`, so some browsers will scroll for the skip link without moving focus.
- The empty-filter recovery button will render "Show all 1" once it becomes reachable.
- `_sass/_dashboard.scss:22` defends `auto-fill` over `auto-fit` with a two-example scenario. Nobody reasoned about one, which is what ships.

## Questions to Consider

1. **Is the non-spectral spine perceptible to anyone who hasn't read ADR-0008?** Six equal-width segments tiled edge to edge is the exact geometry the ADR is at pains to distinguish itself from. The defence is a ΔE00 measurement; the reading is "multicolour stripe". Unequal segment widths — or a hairline gap between them — would make the specimen reading legible in the pixels rather than only in the document.
2. **The site's whole justification is "so you can read any two of them against each other." Where is that in the interface?** Nowhere. Which is the real first feature: the third example, or the comparison view? (The third example — but the roadmap should say so out loud, because the design is currently written as if the dashboard were the product.)
3. **Would you rather ship with one example, or not ship?** The interior pages are strong enough to publish today. The dashboard is not, and the dashboard is the domain apex.
4. **Why is `--link-under` the one token whose measured ratio is recorded as a failure and waived rather than replaced?** ADR-0008 rejected an umber ramp on ΔE grounds against pdfminion's sienna. Was a darker *neutral* underline ever on the table? It costs no hue and removes the only genuine accessibility cost of the site's defining decision.
